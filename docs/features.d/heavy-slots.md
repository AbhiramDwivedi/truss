## Fewer runner spawns (reap fold, same-head skip)

- What: the reap after each member spawn rides on the crew's next factory call as `--reap-first <slug>`; `factory.ts` reaps `<slug>-check` before running the command and adds `reapedFirst: {error}` to its output only when that reap failed. The crew-end reap rides on the late-reply read, the crew's last factory call, and runs as a runner call of its own only when a reap is still due and no call follows (`runCrew`). Green, owns-check and the tests check skip a re-run while the crew head has not moved since they passed. A stubbed crew with a stress fix went from 32 runner spawns to 24 (26 since ADR-0046 item 8 adds the regression tester's admit and integrate).
- What (0.29.0, gates): the checks before QC (green, owns-check, tests check, knowledge lint, QC admit) go out as one runner call, `factory gates --slug S --steps ...`, which runs the due steps in order, stops at the first unmet one and prints each step's own output under `steps` with `gates`, `codes` and `stopped`. stage.js takes each step's result from that call and falls back to the single command when it has none. The same stubbed crew now costs 22 runner spawns; a real crew saves about 4 at the end of its member chain and about 3 per QC fix round.
- What (0.32.0, R-142): the red check, the factory call just before the crew's first member start (the UX direction, else the planner), carries that member's admission as `--admit-first <slug> --admit-label <label> --admit-title <title>` (plus `--admit-persona` and `--admit-high` when the member has them), placed before `--dir` and before heavy's `--`. `admit()` takes its answer from the call's `admittedFirst` and makes no runner call; a held `admittedFirst` stops the crew as a held admit does (`held by admission:`, `BLOCKED: budget:`, `BLOCKED: awaiting client:`). A crew that resumes with saved tasks, or a member start that no factory call precedes, keeps the standalone admit.
