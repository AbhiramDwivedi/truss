## Closeout fixes (0.32.0)

The 0.32.0 closeout crew ran QC and a review after the fact on the two hand-merged 0.30.0 crews, run-lifecycle and crew-decisions. The findings, the deferred wave-4 gaps and the evidence for R-85 to R-98 are in `plugins/truss/docs/goals/0.32.0-closeout.md`. The crew's code changes are the three below.

- **Multi-line product-call text (R-127).** `stage.js` used to flatten every product-call text with `oneLine`, which joins lines with spaces. Text that a person or an agent reads now keeps its lines through `multiLine` in `workflows-src/stage/calls.js`, which collapses blanks inside a line, turns three or more newlines into one blank line, and caps the length. That covers the question in a hold stop (`openCall`), a product owner's `PO: DECIDE` answer (`productCall`), a program manager's inbox note (`takeCall`) and a main-session `change` note (`applyReplies`). Shell arguments (`factory escalate`, `factory crew-decision`), the decision log and learned items stay one line through `oneLine`, because the factory CLI and the log grammar need one line. Tests: the R-127 cases of `tests/stage-bundle.check.ts`.
- **Second run crew state (R-95).** When `plan-write` replaces an existing `plan.json`, it moves the earlier run's `crews/` dir to `run-state/crews` and its `crew-decisions/` dir to `run-state/decisions` under the `plan` lock, in the same step that archives the plan (`cmdPlanWrite` in `bin/factory/plan.ts`). The dir is `run-state-<n>` when `run-state` is taken. Without the move, a new run's crew with an old slug started past its reply checkpoint and listed the old run's crew decisions. Test: `tests/closeout-review-node-032.check.ts`.
- **`pm-question` in the schemas.** `docs/events.schema.json` and `docs/state.schema.json` list `pm-question` as an inbox kind, so the escalate event of a `pm-question` item validates. Test: `tests/closeout-review-node-032.check.ts`.
